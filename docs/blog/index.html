How often does a new post go up?

A new post goes up about once a week, and only when there is something worth saying. A week with no strong topic gets no post rather than a thin one. If you would rather not check back, call or text 215-259-8304 and we will send the next one to you.

An AI agent drafts each note and files it for review, the same way the site auditor files its weekly report. Greg Quinn, the owner, edits every draft and verifies every fact in it, and nothing goes up without his approval.
