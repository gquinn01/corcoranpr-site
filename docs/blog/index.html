ABOUT THESE NOTES, AND ABOUT WORKING TOGETHER

How often does a new post go up?

A new post goes up about once a week, and only when there is something worth saying. A week with no strong topic gets no post rather than a thin one. If you would rather not check back, call or text 215-259-8304 and we will send the next one to you.

Who writes these?

Greg Quinn, the owner, writes them. An AI agent drafts each one and files it for review, the same way the site auditor files its weekly report, and nothing goes up until Greg has checked every fact in it himself.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No, monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. Anything we built or set up for you is yours to keep. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking the door.
