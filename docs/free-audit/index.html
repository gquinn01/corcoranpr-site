IN PLAIN ENGLISHA website audit is a checkup: it finds what is helping your site get found and trusted, what is hurting it, and what to fix first.

What We Check

SIX THINGS THAT DECIDE WHETHER YOU GET FOUND AND CALLED

Search basics

Titles, descriptions, and headings, page by page. The question is whether Google can tell in one pass what each page is for and who it serves. A page with no description, or with three headings competing to be the main one, is a page that is guessing.

AI readiness

Structured data an assistant can actually read, real questions answered in plain language on the page, and whether your robots file welcomes the AI crawlers or quietly blocks them. Some templates block them by default and nobody tells the owner.

Speed and mobile

We open your site on a phone, on a normal connection, and time it. Then we use it the way a customer would. If it is slow to load or awkward to tap, very little else on this list will save it.

Trust signals

Contact details that are present, correct, and the same on every page. Real photographs instead of stock, with alt text that describes them. Small things, and they are what a stranger uses to decide you are a real business.

Local presence

Your Google Business Profile, whether your name, address, and phone number match everywhere they appear, and whether a reader can tell in one look which towns you actually serve.

Conversion

Whether the phone number is obvious on a phone, whether every page has one clear next step, and whether the form on your site actually delivers. Visitors who cannot find how to reach you are not leads.

What You Get

THREE THINGS, IN WRITING, WITHIN ONE BUSINESS DAY

A plain-English report

A score, what is working, what is not, and what to fix first, in the order we would fix it. Written so you can read it yourself and act on it yourself, with no jargon and nothing you need us to interpret.

Repair or replace, answered

Sometimes a site needs a handful of fixes and sometimes it needs replacing. We tell you which one it is and why, including when the answer is that what you have is fine and your money belongs somewhere else.

A flat quote, only if you want one

If you want the work done, you get one number in writing, agreed before anything starts. If you do not, the report is still yours and that is the end of it. You never have to ask for a price to get the read.
