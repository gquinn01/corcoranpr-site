Questions, Answered

WHAT PROSPECTIVE CLIENTS ASK US FIRST

What services does Corcoran Communications offer?

Corcoran Communications offers AI-built websites, automated SEO and AEO monitoring, lead generation, Google Ads, social media advertising, and content marketing. You can take it as one integrated growth program or as individual services.

What is AEO, and why does my business need it?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is getting your business named in the answers AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI give when customers ask for recommendations. A growing share of searches now end without a click, so the businesses inside the AI's answer win. We build and monitor for both classic search and AI answers.

Who will actually work on my account?

Greg Quinn, the owner. He's a U.S. Navy veteran with a staff of AI agents that monitor your site, your search rankings, and Google's algorithm changes around the clock. One senior marketer, no junior account teams, and you can see the agents' reports yourself.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.

What areas does Corcoran Communications serve?

We're based in Quakertown, PA and serve businesses across Bucks and Montgomery Counties and the Greater Lehigh Valley, from Doylestown to Bethlehem, plus longtime clients throughout Pennsylvania, New York, and New Jersey.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

Not necessarily. The free audit tells us whether your current site is worth repairing or holding you back. Some sites need a handful of fixes; some cost less to rebuild than to rescue. Either way you'll see the evidence before you decide anything.

How long does a website take?

Most builds go live within two to four weeks, and you see progress along the way, not a big reveal at the end. Repairs to an existing site often take less. The audit comes first either way, within one business day.

Won't an AI-built website look generic?

Not the way we build them. AI does the heavy lifting, but every site is directed, edited, and fact-checked by a human marketer, in your voice, with your real story. The speed comes from the machines. The judgment doesn't.

Call or text 215-259-8304, or email greg@corcoranpr.com. We'll start with a free AI-powered audit of your current website so you can see exactly what's helping you, what's hurting you, and what it's worth fixing.
