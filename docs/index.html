What services does Corcoran Communications offer?

Corcoran Communications offers AI-built websites, automated SEO and AEO monitoring, lead generation, Google Ads, social media advertising, and content marketing. You can take it as one integrated growth program or as individual services.

What is AEO, and why does my business need it?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is getting your business named in the answers AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI give when customers ask for recommendations. A growing share of searches now end without a click, so the businesses inside the AI's answer win. We build and monitor for both classic search and AI answers.
