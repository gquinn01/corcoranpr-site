The Problems We Solve

WHERE SHOP MARKETING BREAKS DOWN

Invisible in the map results

Most repair searches end at the map pack, three shops and their ratings. If your listing is thin, unclaimed, or inconsistent, you are not in the running. We fix the listing, the categories, the photos, and the details Google uses to rank shops nearby.

Reviews left to chance

For a shop, ratings are the whole first impression. We set up a simple, honest system that asks satisfied customers for a review at the right moment and helps you respond to the bad ones like a professional.

Depending on the insurance pipeline

Referral work from insurers keeps the bays busy and margins thin. Direct customers who chose you pay better and stay loyal. We build the presence that brings them to you on their own.

A phone number nobody can find

On a phone, at the roadside, a customer wants one thing: a number to tap. Every page we build puts it where a thumb already is, and we track which searches and ads made it ring.

Ads that pay for tire kickers

Search ads for repair pull in price shoppers, DIY researchers, and people three counties away. We tighten the geography and cut the wasted searches, so the budget buys customers who can drive to you.

A website from another decade

Slow, tiny text, no mobile layout, and photos from the old ownership. That is what most shop sites look like, and it costs trust in the first second. We fix it, fast, and you own the result.

Questions, Answered

WHAT SHOP OWNERS ASK US FIRST

What matters most in marketing an auto repair or collision shop?

Being found in the map results and trusted at a glance. That means a complete and accurate Google Business Profile, a steady flow of honest reviews, real photos, and a fast website with the phone number impossible to miss. Ads help for urgent searches, but the listing and the reviews do most of the work.

How do you get more reviews for a shop?

With a simple system, not a gimmick: ask satisfied customers at the right moment, make it a one-tap process, and respond to every review, including the bad ones, like a professional. We never write, buy, or fake reviews, and we help you avoid the practices that get listings penalized.

Can marketing reduce our dependence on insurance referral work?

Over time, yes. A strong local presence brings in customers who chose you directly, who tend to pay better and come back. It does not replace referral relationships, but it gives you a second stream you control.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.
