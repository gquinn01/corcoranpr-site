The Problems We Solve

WHERE CREDIT UNION MARKETING GOES WRONG

Losing local searches to national banks

When someone in your county searches for an auto loan, the big banks outspend you. But they cannot out-local you. We win the searches that include your towns, your branches, and the words a neighbor uses.

Membership rules that confuse the ad system

Field-of-membership limits mean plenty of eager clickers cannot join. We build the targeting and the exclusions around who is actually eligible, so the budget reaches people who can walk in the door.

Product campaigns that stop at the click

A rate promotion that sends people to a generic homepage wastes the rate. We build the landing page for the product, with the application one step away and tracking on every step.

Compliance treated as a reason to say nothing

Financial advertising has rules, and we build campaigns and pages that respect them. That is a discipline, not an excuse. Compliant copy can still be clear, warm, and specific.

Branches Google cannot find

Every branch needs a clean listing with matching name, address, and hours, or the map results skip you. This is unglamorous work, and it decides whether you appear when someone searches near a branch.

Reporting nobody on the board can read

Impressions and reach tell a board nothing. We report applications, funded loans where the data allows, and cost per new member, in language a volunteer director can read in five minutes.

Questions, Answered

WHAT MARKETING DIRECTORS AND CEOS ASK US

How is marketing a credit union different from marketing a bank?

You cannot outspend the national banks, but you can out-local them. Credit union marketing wins on geography, membership eligibility, and trust: showing up for the searches near your branches, speaking to the people who can actually join, and telling the member-owned story plainly. That is where the budget goes.

Can you work within financial advertising compliance rules?

Yes. Every campaign and landing page is built with financial advertising rules in mind, from disclosures to rate language, and your compliance review is part of our process before anything goes live. Compliant does not have to mean vague.

How do you measure results for a credit union?

Applications started and completed, new accounts opened, and cost per new member, with funded-loan tracking where your systems allow it. Impressions and reach are inputs, not results, and the report says so in plain English.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking the door.
