The Problems We Solve

WHERE LEGAL MARKETING LOSES MONEY

The most expensive clicks in local advertising

Legal search terms cost more per click than almost anything else. Waste here hurts fast. We build tight campaigns around your actual practice areas and geography, and cut the searches that were never going to become cases.

Intake that leaks cases

A firm can win the search and lose the client to a slow callback or an unanswered form. We wire the tracking and the follow-up so every inquiry gets a fast response and every case traces back to what produced it.

Advertising rules treated as a wall

Attorney advertising has real ethical rules, and we build inside them: no promises of outcomes, proper disclaimers, careful language about results and specialization. Compliant marketing can still be clear and persuasive.

A website that reads like a brochure

Most firm sites list practice areas and partner bios and say nothing a worried person actually needs. We build pages that answer the client's real questions in plain English, which is what both Google and AI assistants reward.

Invisible for the searches that matter

Being found for your firm's name is not marketing. Being found for the problem a client types, in your county, is. We build the local presence and the pages that win those searches, and the AI answers that increasingly replace them.

Reports full of vanity numbers

Rankings and impressions never billed an hour. We report consultations booked, clients retained, and cost per case, in plain language a managing partner can read between hearings.

Questions, Answered

WHAT MANAGING PARTNERS ASK US FIRST

What makes law firm marketing different?

Law firm marketing differs in three ways: legal clicks are among the most expensive in local advertising, so waste hurts fast; attorney advertising has ethical rules that shape every word; and the client is usually anxious and deciding quickly. Good legal marketing respects all three: precise campaigns, compliant language, and pages that answer real questions plainly.

Do you follow attorney advertising rules?

Yes, we build campaigns and pages inside the advertising rules that apply to attorneys, with careful language about results and specialization and the required disclaimers. Your review is part of the process before anything goes live. We are marketers, not lawyers, so your firm's compliance judgment is final.

How do you measure results for a law firm?

We measure consultations booked, clients retained where your intake systems allow us to see it, and cost per case. We wire tracking so each inquiry traces back to the search, page, or ad that produced it, and report it in plain English.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No, monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. Anything we built or set up for you is yours to keep. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking the door.
