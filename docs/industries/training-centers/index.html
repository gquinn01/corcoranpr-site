The Problems We Solve

WHAT SCHOOL MARKETING GETS WRONG, AND WHAT WE DO INSTEAD

Counting inquiries, not students

Most school marketing celebrates form fills. But a form fill is not tuition. We wire tracking so the ad system learns from actual enrollments and goes looking for more of them.

Paying for people who can never enroll

Licensed trades have eligibility rules, and search ads attract plenty of people who do not meet them, plus treatment seekers, job hunters, and financing shoppers. We cut that traffic ruthlessly, week after week.

One message for two very different students

Career changers want a new start. Licensed professionals want a new revenue stream. They need different words, different pages, and different patience. We build for both.

Mistiming the seasons

For most schools, enrollment moves in waves, with the new year and late summer bringing the surges. We build ahead of the wave instead of reacting to it, and use the quiet months for the work that compounds.

The admissions team knows things the ads never learn

Your counselors know which inquiries were real. We connect that judgment back to the advertising, so the system gets smarter with every cohort.

Accounts nobody maintains

Old campaigns, stale keywords, and budget leaking quietly for years. We clean house and then watch it daily.

Questions, Answered

WHAT SCHOOL OWNERS AND ADMISSIONS DIRECTORS ASK US

What does marketing for a trade school involve?

Everything runs toward enrollment: search ads built around the courses you offer, landing pages that speak to career changers and licensed professionals separately, and tracking that connects every inquiry to what happens in admissions. The goal is not traffic. It is students you can count.

How do you keep ads from wasting money on people who cannot enroll?

Licensed trades attract searchers who do not qualify, plus people looking for treatments, jobs, or financing rather than training. We maintain aggressive exclusion lists and review the actual search terms every week, so the budget concentrates on people who can actually sit in your classroom.

How do you measure success for a school?

Cost per enrollment, not clicks or impressions. We connect your ads to your admissions records so you can see which campaigns produce students and what each one costs. You get the report in plain English, and the numbers come from your own records.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.
