What Blue Bell Businesses Need From a Website

SIX KINDS OF WORK ON THE CORRIDOR, AND THE BAR EACH ONE MEETS

Advisory

Professional and advisory firms

Consulting, legal, and financial buyers arrive already skeptical and already comparing. The page has to say who you serve best, what engagement actually looks like, and who does the work. Vagueness reads as inexperience here.

B2B

Suppliers to the corporate offices

If your customer is a company rather than a household, the site is a qualification document. Capability, scale, references, and process, laid out so a procurement decision can be made without a meeting first.

Specialists

Practices whose patients do the research

A specialist practice here competes on credentials and convenience together. Who the patient will see, what the process is, and booking that works from a work laptop between meetings.

Speed

Faster and clearer than a bigger budget

Your visitor has just come from a corporate site with a real budget behind it. You do not need their budget. You do need to load faster and be clearer, and clarity is the cheaper of the two to buy.

Daytime

Businesses researched during work hours

A lot of the searching here happens on a desktop, at a desk, between other tasks. That means a page that reads well on a wide screen, states the point early, and does not hide the next step behind a scroll.

Corridor

Firms working Ambler and the 202 stretch

A business on this corridor rarely serves only the town it sits in. Name the towns along the stretch that you actually cover, or Google will hand the click to a competitor who does.
