What Lansdale Businesses Need From a Website

SIX KINDS OF BUSINESS HERE, AND WHAT EACH ONE HAS TO PROVE

Industry

Manufacturers and suppliers

Buyers here shortlist before they call, often from outside the area entirely. What they want on the page is capability, capacity, tolerances, and who you already supply. Personality can wait until the meeting.

Trades

Contractors covering North Penn and the Indian Valley

If you work a radius rather than an address, the towns you cover belong on the page in plain words. A homeowner in Harleysville needs to see their own town named before they will believe you turn up.

Practices

Medical and dental practices

Patients here have several options within a short drive, so the decision turns on the small print: who they will see, what the first visit involves, which plans you take, and whether they can book without phoning at lunchtime.

Downtown

Shops and restaurants in town

A downtown business lives or dies on whether the details online are current. Hours, parking, and what is actually available today. An out-of-date page turns a customer away more reliably than a closed door.

Reputation

Family businesses decades old

A long reputation is an asset until the search results do not reflect it. Reviews, a complete listing, and a site that looks maintained are what carry forty years of goodwill into a search someone runs tonight.

Orbit

Businesses serving the towns around it

A Lansdale business often pulls from Souderton, Harleysville, and North Wales too. Say so on the page. Google will not assume your reach, and neither will the person deciding whether you are near enough.
