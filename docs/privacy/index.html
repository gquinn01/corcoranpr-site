Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page says what we collect, who touches it, and how to get it deleted. Last updated August 27, 2026.

What the Form Collects

SIX FIELDS, FOUR OF THEM REQUIRED

The only thing this site collects is what you type into the audit request form. That is your name, your business name, your website address, and your email address. A phone number and a message are optional, and the form sends without them.

There is nothing else. No account to create, no mailing list, no hidden fields except one empty box that never shows on screen and exists to catch spam bots.

Who Handles It

YOUR BROWSER, FORMSPREE, OUR INBOX

The form posts to Formspree, a hosted form service, which delivers the submission to greg@corcoranpr.com. That is the whole path: your browser, Formspree, our inbox. This site has no database and no server code of ours behind it.

What We Do With It

ANSWER YOU, AND RUN THE AUDIT YOU ASKED FOR

We use what you send to answer you and to run the audit you asked for. That means looking at the website address you gave us, scanning it, and emailing you the report. If you left a phone number, we may call or text you about it.

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, and we do not hand it to anyone else for their own use.

Cookies and Analytics

ONE TOOL, COUNTING VISITS

This site runs Google Analytics. It sets cookies in your browser so we can measure how many people visit, which pages they read, where they came from, and how many fill in the audit form.

Analytics does not receive what you typed. It records that a form was sent, not your name, your email, or your message. If you would rather not be counted, your browser's cookie settings turn it off, and Google publishes an analytics opt-out add-on that does the same.

How to Have It Deleted

ONE EMAIL OR ONE PHONE CALL, NO REASON REQUIRED

Email greg@corcoranpr.com, or call or text 215-259-8304, and say you want your information deleted. We remove the email and the copy Formspree holds, and we tell you when it is done. You do not have to give a reason.

If what we do with your information changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves.

Questions, Answered

THE THREE PEOPLE ACTUALLY ASK

No, we do not sell your information, rent it, or hand it to anyone else for their own use. The only company that touches your form submission is Formspree, which delivers it to our inbox.

Email greg@corcoranpr.com, or call or text 215-259-8304, and say you want it deleted. We remove the email and the copy Formspree holds, and we tell you when it is done. You do not have to give a reason.

Google Analytics sets cookies so we can measure visits, traffic sources, and form fills. It does not receive your name, your email, or anything else you typed into the form. Nothing else on this site sets a cookie.
