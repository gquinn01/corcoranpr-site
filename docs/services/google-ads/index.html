What We Manage

TIGHT CAMPAIGNS, HONEST NUMBERS, NO SET AND FORGET

Intent

Only the searches worth paying for

We build the keyword list from what a paying customer actually types, then spend as much time on the negative list: the searches that look close but never buy. That is where most local ad budgets quietly go.

Local

Campaigns that stop at your service area

Radius, towns, and hours set to where and when you can actually take the work, so you are not paying to reach someone two counties away at midnight.

Landing

The page the click lands on

An ad can only do so much if the page it opens is slow or vague. We make sure the landing page answers the promise in the ad and puts the phone number where a thumb already is.

Tracking

You know which ad made the phone ring

Call and form tracking wired up from the start, so the reporting is about booked work instead of impressions. If a campaign is not producing calls, you will see it plainly.

Waste

Spend checked daily

Automated watchdog scripts run inside the ad account and check spend and tracking every day. AI agents review the search terms and performance every week, and a human decides what changes. Small leaks get closed before they become a month you cannot get back.

Reporting

Plain English, every month

What we spent, what it brought in, what we changed, and what we are doing next. Written so you can read it between jobs, not so it looks impressive.

Built for Lead Quality, Not Click Counts

A CLICK IS A COST. A BOOKED JOB IS THE POINT.

Small budgets punish waste

A national advertiser can afford to buy a thousand wrong clicks to find the right one. A local business cannot. So the work here is mostly subtraction: cutting the searches that were never going to call, the hours nobody answers the phone, and the places you do not serve. What is left is the traffic worth paying for.

Watched every day, not every quarter

Campaigns drift. Competitors change bids, a search term starts leaking money, a landing page breaks. Automated watchdog scripts inside the ad account check spend and tracking every day. AI agents review the search terms and performance every week, and a human decides what actually changes. You get the plain-English version, not a wall of metrics.

Questions, Answered

WHAT PEOPLE ASK BEFORE THEY SPEND A DOLLAR ON ADS

Are Google Ads worth it for a small local business?

Sometimes, and not always. Ads work when someone is actively searching for what you sell and you can take the work when they call. If your website is not ready to convert the click, ads just make a slow leak faster, and we will tell you so. That is exactly what the free audit is for.

How much should I spend?

That depends on what one customer is worth to you and what the competition in your trade is bidding locally. We work it out with you before anything launches, you set the ceiling, and nothing goes live until you have agreed the number in writing.

How will I know whether the ads are working?

Because we track the calls and the form submissions, not just the clicks. You get a monthly report in plain English showing what was spent, what came in, and what we changed. If a campaign is not paying for itself, that will be in the report too.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.
