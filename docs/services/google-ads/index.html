Questions, Answered

WHAT PEOPLE ASK BEFORE THEY SPEND A DOLLAR ON ADS

Are Google Ads worth it for a small local business?

Google Ads are worth it for some local businesses and not others. Ads work when someone is actively searching for what you sell and you can take the work when they call. If your website is not ready to convert the click, ads just make a slow leak faster, and we will tell you so. That is exactly what the free audit is for.

How much should I spend?

What you should spend depends on what one customer is worth to you and what the competition in your trade is bidding locally. A national advertiser can afford to buy a thousand wrong clicks to find the right one. A local business cannot, so on a smaller budget the work is mostly subtraction, cutting everything that was never going to call before it spends your money. We work it out with you before anything launches, you set the ceiling, and nothing goes live until you have agreed on the number in writing.

How will I know whether the ads are working?

You will know because we track the calls and the form submissions, not just the clicks. You get a monthly report in plain English showing what was spent, what it booked, and what we changed. If a campaign is not paying for itself, that will be in the report too.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No, monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. Anything we built or set up for you is yours to keep. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking the door.
