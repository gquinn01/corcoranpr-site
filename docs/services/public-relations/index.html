Questions, Answered

WHAT OWNERS ASK BEFORE THEIR FIRST CAMPAIGN

What does a PR firm actually do for a small business?

It finds the stories in your business, gets them in front of local and industry press, plans launches and events people show up for, and makes sure the coverage keeps working on your website and profiles. The result is credibility you cannot buy with ads.

Can PR help with Google and AI search?

Yes, and increasingly so. When other people write about your business, search engines and AI assistants read it as evidence you are real and worth recommending. We treat earned coverage as part of your search and answer-engine strategy, not a separate thing.

Do we need a big budget for PR?

No. We scope PR to the story and the moment, whether that is one launch or an ongoing program, and quote it flat. The free audit is where we tell you honestly whether PR is the right next move for your business.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.
