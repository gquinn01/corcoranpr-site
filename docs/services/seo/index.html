Ranking Is Half the Job. You Also Have to Be the Answer.

ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION, THE PART MOST FIRMS ARE NOT DOING YET

What changed

More and more people stop asking Google for a list of links and start asking ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Google's own AI who they should call. Those answers name a handful of businesses. If you are not one of them, the search is over before your website ever gets a chance.

What we do about it

Answer engine optimization, or AEO, is the work of getting your business named in those answers. That means structured data an AI can read, questions answered in plain language on the page, an entity tied cleanly to your real profiles, and a site that welcomes the AI crawlers instead of blocking them. Then our agents check weekly that it is still true.

Questions, Answered

WHAT PROSPECTIVE CLIENTS ASK US FIRST

How long does SEO take to work?

It is not a switch, and anyone who promises you page one next week is selling something. Technical fixes can show up in weeks, and competitive terms take longer. What we will put in writing is our promise: in 90 days AI assistants are telling customers to call you, or we keep working for free. In the meantime you get a report every week, so you can see movement instead of taking our word for it.

What is AEO, and why does my business need it?

AEO, or answer engine optimization, is getting your business named in the answers AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google's AI give when customers ask for recommendations. A growing share of searches now end without a click, so the businesses inside the AI's answer win. We build and monitor for both classic search and AI answers.

What makes you different from other SEO companies?

Two things. You work with the owner, a senior marketer with 26 years in the business, not a junior account team you never meet. And you see the agents' raw reports yourself every week, so the work is visible instead of described. We have kept 98% of our clients, which is the number we would want to see if we were hiring an SEO company.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. We keep clients by delivering results they can read in their own reports, not by locking the door.
