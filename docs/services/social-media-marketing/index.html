Questions, Answered

WHAT PEOPLE ASK BEFORE HANDING OVER THEIR ACCOUNTS

Which platforms do you handle?

Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. We would rather do two of them properly than spread you thin across six, so we start where your customers actually are.

Is this posting, or is it paid advertising?

Both, run as one thing. Regular posts keep the account alive and credible; paid campaigns put the good ones in front of people who have never heard of you.

Do you write the posts, or do I?

We do. You give us the raw material, the photos from the job, what happened, what you want people to know, and we turn it into posts in your voice. Everything is yours to approve, and we never invent a customer, a review, or a result.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Monitoring and marketing plans run month to month, and you can walk anytime. Anything we built or set up for you is yours to keep. We keep clients by delivering results, not by locking the door.
