Questions, Answered

WHAT PEOPLE ASK BEFORE THEY COMMIT TO A BUILD

How long does website design take?

Most builds go live within two to four weeks, and you see progress along the way, not a big reveal at the end. Repairs to an existing site often take less. The audit comes first either way, within one business day.

Do I own the website when it is finished?

Yes. You own the site outright. There is no page-builder rental, and for most builds no monthly platform fee. If you ever want to take it somewhere else, you can, and you leave with everything.

I already have a website. Do I have to start over?

Not necessarily. The free audit tells us whether your current site is worth repairing or holding you back. Some sites need a handful of fixes; some cost less to rebuild than to rescue. Either way you'll see the evidence before you decide anything.

Where are you based, and do you work with businesses near me?

We are in Quakertown, PA, so if you have been searching for a web designer near me anywhere in upper Bucks County, Montgomery County, or the Greater Lehigh Valley, we are probably close by. We work with clients across Pennsylvania, New York, and New Jersey too.
